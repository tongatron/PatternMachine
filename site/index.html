Playing: keys and controls

Playing

  • 1234 QWER ASDF ZXCV play the first 16 rows.
  • space play/stop; L turns the Pattern/Song loop on or off.
  • With Rec on, whatever you play goes into the pattern, quantized to the step.
  • cmd+Z undo, cmd+shift+Z redo.

Steps

  • Click = on/off.
  • Tab enters the grid; use the arrow keys to move between steps and instruments.
  • Right-click = menu to choose normal, accent, ghost or off; alt+click = accent.
  • shift+click = ghost note (soft).
  • Probability, Ratchets, Flam, Tuning, Decay and Filter (above the grid): in the selected mode, clicking an active step changes that hit only.

Rows

  • Grp = choke group (hi‑hats cut each other off).
  • Tune = row tuning, in semitones.
  • Len. makes the row loop with fewer steps (polyrhythms).
  • ⚙ opens the voice parameters: Decay shortens the tail, Filter+Reso are the 4-pole low-pass like the VCFs on the machine's outputs, Start moves the sample start, Rev plays it reversed.
  • Feel (in the ⚙ panel) pushes the row's hits ahead or behind, from −30 to +30 ms: snare “laid back”, kick “pushed”.
  • The Mixer tab shows every row as a strip with fader, pan, meter, M and S, then the drums, synth and master; the master limiter keeps the mix under 0 dB.
  • Kit next to a row's sound takes that row from another machine (a TR-808 kick under an SP-1200 kit): the row keeps it when you change the selected machine.
  • In the Sequencer, Mute on a block silences the rows you choose in that block only (a break without the hat, an intro without the kick).

Groove and machine

  • MPC-style Swing (50–75%) and Humanize next to the BPM.
  • Machine changes the sounds: E-mu SP-1200 at 16 or 12 bit, Yamaha RX-5, Roland TR-808, TR-909, TR-707, TR-727, TR-606, CR-78, CR-8000, Linn LinnDrum, Oberheim DMX, E-mu Drumulator, Sequential DrumTraks, Simmons SDS-V. Rows using a sound the other machine lacks move to the closest equivalent.

Patterns and generator

  • ✦ Vary (Pattern panel) transforms the pattern you are editing, even one you wrote by hand; cmd+Z undoes it.
  • ✦ Generate opens the library and generator: it shows 8 suggestions for the selected machine and style right away, from the busiest to the simplest (the ● dots show how interesting each one is).
  • Tempo in the library: by default the suggestions stay within ±5 BPM of the project tempo, the closest first; Near… lets you type another tempo, Any tempo turns the search off. With Random only the styles that reach that tempo are used.
  • On every pattern: Similar creates 4 close variants; Vary transforms it in 20 ways (sparse, half-time, open hat, new fill, percussion layer, stop… and five hybrids with voices from another style).
  • Arrange derives intro, chorus, break and outro; Lock keeps some voices and regenerates the others.
  • Every pattern has a code: the same code regenerates the same pattern.
  • In the library: choosing a style, section or length immediately brings 8 new suggestions in place of the previous ones; ↻ More suggestions generates another 8 with the same settings, × removes a pattern from the list. In the Favorites tab, Clear favorites removes them all.

PATTERN-MACHINE as an app too

For Mac, Windows and Linux. Compared with the site in a browser:

  • Drag the pattern or song (MIDI or WAV) straight onto the DAW timeline, with no zip to unpack.
  • Live MIDI output: the "PatternMachine" port plays Drum Kit Designer, Drum Machine Designer or any instrument, and can be recorded.
  • Follows the DAW clock: starts, stops, jumps and changes tempo together with Logic.
  • Projects as files in Music › PatternMachine, easy to copy or back up.
  • Works offline, with no network or password.

Why sign up

You are using PATTERN-MACHINE without an account. Sign up for free and:

  • Download the app for macOS, Windows and Linux, with advanced features: drag MIDI and WAV into your DAW, live MIDI output, sync to the DAW clock, projects as files and offline use.
