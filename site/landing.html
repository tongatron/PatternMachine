Features

✦

Pattern generator

67 styles in 10 families, from punk and post-punk to hip hop, dub, DnB, disco and club. Generate 8 suggestions, filter them by tempo, save favorites and use a code to regenerate the same pattern later.

▦

Step grid

8–36 steps, 4/4 or 6/8, straight or triplet grids and 40–240 BPM. Add normal hits, accents and ghost notes, then edit probability, ratchets, flam, tuning, decay or filter on one hit at a time.

≈

Groove & feel

Choose straight, MPC swing, shuffle, laid back, pushed or Dilla pocket. Add humanize globally, shorten a row for polyrhythms and nudge its hits ahead or behind with per-row Feel.

⇆

Variations

Try sparse, half-time, new fill, open hat, percussion layer, stop and syncopated kick variations. Similar makes close alternatives; Vary transforms the pattern, and undo brings the original back.

♫

Song mode

Chain patterns into intro, verse, chorus, break and outro blocks with their own repeats. Drag to reorder, play from any point, loop a block and mute selected rows inside one block for real breaks.

●

Live view

Just the grid, Play, Rec, tempo and metronome. Play from the keyboard or the pads and record hits while the pattern runs.

∿

Synthesizer beta

A synth part next to the drums, with its own engines, presets, patterns and notes. Generate a bass line, choose a key and scale, edit notes, accents and slides, and arrange it in its own song lane.

◌

Sampler & sound shaping

Import or record local sounds, add rows to the grid and shape each voice with decay, filter, resonance, sample start, reverse and tuning. Changes are saved with the project.

≡

Mixer

A strip for every row with fader, pan, meter, mute and solo, then drums, synth and master. Peak hold, resettable meters and a limiter keep the master under 0 dB in playback and the mix export.

⌨

MIDI keyboards

Keys play the synth with velocity, pads trigger drums and transport, knobs move any parameter. Ready-made maps for AKAI MPK mini Play and Arturia MiniLab 3, plus Learn. How it works →

⇪

Export anywhere

Export a pattern or song as Logic-mapped MIDI, General MIDI or multitrack MIDI, plus WAV and MP3. Build ready-made packages for Logic, Ableton Live, REAPER and other DAWs.

⇄

Import

Drop in MIDI, audio drum loops, drum tabs, PatternTXT, Hydrogen, REAPER, Ableton Live, MusicXML or a PATTERN-MACHINE JSON project. Kick, snare and hats can be detected from a loop.

☁

Projects everywhere

Saved projects live in your account: the same in every browser, on your phone and in the desktop app. Saving works offline and syncs later; if a project changed elsewhere, your version is kept as a copy.

◐

Made to play

Light and dark themes styled after the SP-1200 front panel, 80 levels of undo and a metronome that runs even with the transport stopped.

MIDI keyboards

Plug in a keyboard and play.

Connect a USB MIDI keyboard, open the MIDI tab and press Connect. It works in Chrome, Edge and Firefox (Safari can't read MIDI devices) and in the desktop app.

  • Keys play the synth with velocity; with Rec on, what you play is written into the pattern.
  • Pads play the drum rows or run the transport: Play, solo drums or synth, Rec, tap tempo, metronome, previous and next pattern.
  • Knobs and faders move volumes, BPM, swing, humanize or any synth parameter: cutoff, resonance, envelopes and effects.
  • Learn: press it on a pad or knob, then hit the pad or turn the knob to assign it. Each keyboard keeps its own map, recognized by name, and two can be used at once.
