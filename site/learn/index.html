Learn drum programming

Short, hands-on lessons with a drum machine right on the page. You'll learn how beats are built, how to program them step by step, how to play them with your keyboard, and how to put it all into practice in PATTERN-MACHINE.

  1. ListenEvery idea starts as a sound: you hear it before you read about it.
  2. Try itProgram it on a small grid that works like the real one, with instant checks.
  3. Play itUse your keyboard like a pad controller and record in time.
  4. In the appDo it in PATTERN-MACHINE, with a mission card that guides you.

The course

Lessons appear here as they are published. Your progress is kept in this browser.

The keys, at a glance

1row 12row 23row 34row 4 Qrow 5Wrow 6Erow 7Rrow 8 Arow 9Srow 10Drow 11Frow 12 Zrow 13Xrow 14Crow 15Vrow 16
  • space Play / Stop.
  • 1…V play the first 16 rows of the Drum Grid, top to bottom. With Rec on while it plays, what you play is written on the nearest step.
  • Click a step: on/off · alt+click: accent · shift+click: ghost note.
  • Tab enters the grid, the arrow keys move, Enter turns a step on or off.
  • ⌘Z / ⌘⇧Z undo and redo (Ctrl on Windows and Linux).

The full list is in the Guide. Keyboard playing has its own level in the course.
