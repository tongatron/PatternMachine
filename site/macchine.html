Bit depth and sample rate: why digital machines sound different

The first sampling machines had little memory: they recorded with fewer bits (fewer volume levels, so more noise and “grit”) and at a lower sample rate (less top end and a metallic haze). The SP-1200 works at 12 bit and about 26 kHz, the LinnDrum at 8 bit, the TR-909 cymbals at 6 bit; a CD is 16 bit and 44.1 kHz. Here a clean sound goes through the same reductions.
