Three ways to make a note

KORG and Custom in the Engine menu

PatternMachine AudioWorklet

The native engine runs in an AudioWorklet, separate from the page thread. It was written for the project so the sequencer can schedule notes ahead of time, keep the timing steady and render the same sound offline for WAV/MP3 export.

Tone.js · optional engine

A ready-made Web Audio instrument layer

Tone.js is a local, MIT-licensed library. PatternMachine uses its PolySynth, oscillator, envelope and filter nodes for live notes, the computer keyboard, MIDI input and sequencer playback. It has its own preset bank — Tone Soft Pad, Tone Square Bass, Tone Triangle Pluck and Tone Sine Lead — and its own smaller set of controls. Select it from Engine → Tone.js in the Synth panel.

What is not an emulation

A sound family, not a museum replica

The Synth panel does not claim to reproduce one complete commercial synthesizer. The Custom engine is a compact subtractive instrument inspired by the signal flow of instruments such as the Korg minilogue family; its 303-style glide is a playing method. Tone.js is a general-purpose library, not a model of a particular machine.

Official KORG sounds · NTS-1 mkII logue units

Choose Engine → KORG in the Synth panel: the oscillator is a KORG logue unit, built with KORG's official logue SDK for the NTS-1 mkII and compiled to WebAssembly. The presets KORG waves and KORG pluck start from here.

The units are listed in engine/logue/units.json (in the Custom engine they are the KORG logue unit oscillator type). At the moment the site ships two:

waves.wasm

Waves

The NTS-1 mkII oscillator unit, compiled from KORG's logue SDK to standalone WebAssembly. It supplies the oscillator while PatternMachine supplies the voice, filter, envelopes, modulation and effects around it.

pluck.wasm

Pluck

A physical-model / plucked-string style oscillator from the same SDK. It is loaded per voice and resampled when the browser runs at 44.1 kHz instead of the unit's native 48 kHz.

The list is deliberately data-driven: adding another compiled .wasm unit and rebuilding units.json makes it appear in the menu after reload.
